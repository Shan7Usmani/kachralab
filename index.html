04 / THE RULE

Explainable, not inferred.

EXPLAINABLE RISK ENGINE
Risk = ( 0.35R + 0.25H + 0.25P + 0.15D ) × M 1.00
30 m

Spatial memory

Reports inside a 30 metre geodesic radius merge into the same persistent case. Recurrence increases; the site does not get a fresh ticket.

150 m

Spillover halo

If waste returns or moves inside the 150 metre halo, the system flags, escalates and re-investigates. Sweeping the pile elsewhere is not a pass.

×1.35

Monsoon surge

Rain multiplies risk because leachate travels faster into water tables and drains. The score is capped at 100; the raw value stays visible.
